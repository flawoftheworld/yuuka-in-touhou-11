Yuuka Kazami in Touhou 11

A fan-made Touhou 11 character replacement patch.

Features

Replaces the playable character's sprites with Yuuka Kazami.

Includes custom stage dialogue changes.

Requirements:

- A legally obtained (or not) copy of Touhou 11: Subterranean Animism.

- Touhou Community Reliant Automatic Patcher (thcrap).

Installation

Back up your existing thcrap configuration.

Copy the yuuka_th11 patch folder into your thcrap repos/local/ directory.

Add repos/local/yuuka_th11/ to the patches array in your run configuration, after the English translation patch.

Launch Touhou 11 using that configuration.

Notes

This is an unofficial fan-made mod and is not affiliated with Team Shanghai Alice.
